Advertisements

When one considers that Goldeneye 007 became one of the Nintendo 64's top-selling games, the advertisements for it feel relatively scare, especially prior to release. The advertisements we do have in all forms of media are compiled here. We also have some GoldenEye-adjacent advertisements.

Print Ads

Nintendo Power Volume 93 • February 1997

Fold-out scanned from original Nintendo Power magazine.

N64 Magazine • Issue #5 • August 1997

Advertisement for Edge Magazine's review of GoldenEye in N64 Magazine issue #5.

Gamefan Volume 5 • Issue 10 • October 1997

The screenshots on the right are from an early version of the game and include an alternate title screen.

Nintendo Power Issue 102 • November 1997

GoldenEye themed print advertisement for Nintendo Power and game strategy guides. They remade weapon models and a bit of Cradle for this ad. The blue catwalks and purple sky are pretty wild!
