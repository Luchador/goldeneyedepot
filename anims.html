Animations

This page has videos for all character animations. Goldeneye 007 has 170 animations. Most were created by developer Duncan Botwood with various motion capture setups. Brett Jones cleaned up the animations and hand animated a few such as the jump off the dam.

Animations are listed by their hexadecimal IDs. There are a few gaps in the list such as 32 and 33 where animations were perhaps removed or just never made. These videos were created with the GoldenEye Setup Editor.
