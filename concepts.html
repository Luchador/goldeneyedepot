Concept Art & Sketches

Bond Outfits

Concept sketch for Bond's green tactical outfit used in the Cuba levels and his tuxedo vs. the final models.

Bond and Xenia

A more refined sketch of Bond's tuxedo on the left and a concept for Xenia in the center. I'm not sure what Bond's outfit on the right was intended to be. Maybe a parachute harness? Picture of Xenia below for comparison.

Commando and Russian Soldier

The sketch on the left most closely resembles the Arctic Commando or Jungle Commando wearing a beret. There's a sketch of a Soviet ushanka in the middle, and a Soviet/Russian soldier wearing a pilotka on the right.

Siberian Special Forces and Moonraker Elite

Brett Jones' sketches for what would become the Siberian Special Forces and the Moonraker Elite.
