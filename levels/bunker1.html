Bunker

Mission 2: Severnaya

Part ii: Bunker

Objectives

  1. Disrupt all surveillance equipment (SA / 00A)
  2. Copy Goldeneye key and leave original (A / SA / 00A)
  3. Get personnel to activate computer (00A)
  4. Download data from computer (00A)
  5. Photograph main video screen (A / SA / 00A)

Background

A spy satellite has detected increased levels of activity at an old observatory complex near Severnaya (62.08n, 102.58e) in Russia. Other sources have confirmed the shipment of a large amount of computer hardware to that location, and an influx of skilled personnel.

M

007, I want you to infiltrate this base and find out what the devil's going on there. The satellite photographs reveal that there has been recent underground excavation. We'd like to know the exact nature and planned extent of the workings. By the way, this is a covert operation. You may have to neutralize the security, but try not to be too obvious about it.

Q

You'll be doing some computer hacking on this mission, 007. I know you'd rather be cracking open a bottle of bubbly than cracking passwords, so I've taken the liberty of putting together a little gadget which will do the hard work for you. If you attach the data thief to their mainframe it should override any computer security measures and download the information we require.

Moneypenny

Underground in Siberia, James? Some of us don't get further than the Northern Line.

Description

Bunker is the fifth level of GoldenEye 007 and the last level of the second mission. Its small size makes it stand in contrast to the spacious Surface level prior. In fact Bunker 1 is the smallest level in terms of area, measuring about 42 x 71 meters. That said, I would also consider it the "densest" level in terms of the number of enemies to eliminate and objectives to complete.

The level is comprised of four to seven rooms, depending on how you wish to count them, a couple halls, and the interior of the ventilation tower. The most important room is the main control room. The level has a theme that it is under construction and that is seen in the exposed rock walls and unconnected light fixtures in some areas.

Bunker is well-guarded and loud gunfire will bring a squad of enemies down on you. The biggest threat may be the many different ways alarms can be activated. If an alarm goes off you will have to fight off infinite waves of Janus Special Forces who sport sunglasses, and, more concerningly, body armor.
