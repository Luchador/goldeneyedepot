Bunker 2

Mission 5: Severnaya

Part ii: Bunker

Objectives

  1. Compare staff / casualty lists (SA / 00A)
  2. Recover CCTV tape (A / SA / 00A)
  3. Disable all security cameras (SA / 00A)
  4. Recover Goldeneye operations manual (00A)
  5. Escape with Natalya (A / SA / 00A)

Background

Escape is the first priority. Find the staff and casualty lists, and the CCTV tape of your capture. If there are any witnesses, try and bring them out as well.

M

Unbelievable, 007. If this gets out we'll be the laughing stock of the international community. As soon as you get back - IF you get back - you're getting a performance review. Only completing the objectives will give you a hope of keeping your rather checkered career intact.

Q

You've got your wits and that's about it, 007. Not much I can do to help you here. You might try to find your equipment again when you escape from the cell, though. It took me a lot of time to get all of that stuff together.

Moneypenny

Grabbed by the Spetznaz, James? Sounds painful!
