Description

Complex is a multiplayer arena designed specifically for multiplayer. It is generally a favorite among GoldenEye 007 players. The level is known for its blood-red sky, tight quarters combat, metallic aesthetic, and numerous secret hiding spots. The name Complex comes from the full name of the Aztec level which is "Aztec Complex."

Level Map

Secret Hiding Spots

There are four places in Complex where you can walk through the wall and into small hiding spots. They were added by the level designer, Karl Hilton, who wanted an advantage over his opponents during play testing sessions.

"The secret hiding places in the Complex...that purely came out of me cheating because I was getting my ass handed to me playing the multiplayer...no one could work out how I kept coming out of places they didn't know I was and killing them from behind."

— Karl Hilton, Level Designer (Source)
