Dam

Mission 1: Arkangelsk

Part i: Dam

Objectives

  1. Neutralize all alarms (A / SA)
  2. Install covert modem (A / SA / 00A)
  3. Intercept data backup (A / SA / 00A)
  4. Bungee jump from platform (A)

Background

MI6 has confirmed the existence of a secret chemical warfare facility at the Byelomorye dam, USSR. Its heavily guarded location and workings are a cause for major concern. Nerve gas produced there has been turning up in the hands of hostile regimes around the world and deals with international terrorists have been rumored. This facility should be destroyed with extreme prejudice.

M

The area around the dam is well defended against full scale military attack but a lone agent stands a much better chance. The easiest way to gain entrance to the weapons factory will be to get down the face of the dam. Q had an idea about that.

Q

Information concerning shipping and contacts is stored on a computer system in a secret ops room within the dam. With this covert modem connected to their satellite link we can intercept the data when a backup is carried out. As for getting down the dam, use the bungee rope. At the bottom of the jump, use the piton gun. Simple.

Moneypenny

Hardly a lover's leap, James. Be careful though, I hate to think of you falling for anyone else.

Description

Dam is the first level of GoldenEye 007. No tutorials. No training missions. You're thrown right into the action. The level consists of three large areas bounded by cliffs, a vehicle tunnel, the top of the dam, the tunnels below the dam, and docks behind the dam. Soviet military presence is high. Interspersed in these areas you'll find six guard towers, each with a patrolling guard. There's even a pillbox that houses two guards, making them difficult to hit.

The basic premise of this level is that Bond needs to reach a platform at the top of the dam so that he can bungee jump to the chemical warfare facility located at the base of the dam.

The level takes place in the Arkhangelsk Oblast, northern Russia. The cliffs are snowy and the level has a generally cold and gray feel. The design of the dam itself is based on Contra Dam in Switzerland, formerly called Verzasca Dam. That's where the movie's bungee jump scene was filmed. Unlike the real dam, this one has three guard towers on top — a feature planned even in the Design Document. It also has docks behind it which the real dam does not.

The intro text says this level takes place "nine years ago." If we go by the film's release date in 1995 that would place the mission in 1986.

Level Tour

The Island

Dam's island has long been one of GoldenEye's most well-known mysteries. As soon as the game released players standing on the dam or docks noticed that two strange structures stood all the way across the reservoir. The Sniper Rifle, conveniently available in this level, gave the best look through its wobbling scope.

There is no way to travel to the island in the vanilla game. For awhile this view from afar was the most players could see.

Then GameShark codes came along. Players could noclip across the reservoir and see the island up close.

Turns out this actually isn't an island, but rather a peninsula. Nevertheless the "island" name has persisted.

Those two structures are a drone gun fortification and a guard tower. The drone gun is completely inactive because it is a part of the level's background, not an object. The guard tower has a notably different look than all the others in the level.

A look behind the structures shows how they are connected to the mountains.

The tower's interior is empty except for a ladder running up to the second level.

This ladder cannot be used so we'll mod the game to get a view of the second level.

Here is the second level which is also empty. The windows are the older "beta" style that do not shatter.

Curiosities

1. Garage Hatch

The building with the Russian Commandant has a garage with a roll-up door. In this room there is some kind of hatch in the floor. Its texture is the same as that used on some roll-up doors. GoldenEye doesn't have that many floor hatches which makes this stand out. Was this hatch ever meant for anything more than decoration? Could the bungee equipment have been found down here?

A look beneath using the GoldenEye Setup Editor is a little disappointing. There is nothing under the hatch at all.

2. 2, 3, 4

There are three access points to the tunnels under the dam. You would think they would be numbered 1, 2, and 3, but they are actually numbered 2, 3, and 4. Why? Where is 1?

3. Explosive Crates

The common silver metal crates found throughout GoldenEye's single player are always invincible except for these two at the far end of the dam's docks. Is there some special meaning to this? It is most likely the level designer just forgot to flag them as invincible.

4. Manholes

Each of the three guard towers on the dam has a manhole cover in front of it.

What's really interesting is that if you go down to the tunnels you can see the manholes from underneath!

According to level designer Duncan Botwood on Twitter/X, there was an idea that the player may have been able to open the manhole covers, but this idea was never implemented.

5. Big Oil

The stacked oil drums near the pillbox are huge. They're much larger than any others in the game. Why are they so big? It's a mystery.

6. Long Way Down

There is platform at the bottom of the dam which is difficult to see because the level fades out before you can reach it. This screenshot is from a modded game to give a better look. For more information see this section on the Dam Prerelease page.

Trivia

  • The double gate where the truck eventually stops and waits is an interesting gameplay element but also an important and intentional optimization. It stops certain areas of the level from having to be rendered at the same time. (Source: GoldenEye 007 by Alyse Knorr, p. 104)
