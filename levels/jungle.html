Jungle

Mission 7: Cuba

Part i: Jungle

Objectives

  1. Destroy drone guns (A / SA / 00A)
  2. Eliminate Xenia (A / SA / 00A)
  3. Blow up ammo dump (SA / 00A)
  4. Escort Natalya to Janus base (A / SA / 00A)

Background

Satellite reconnaissance has been unable to locate any installation near the coordinates recovered from the Janus computers in St. Petersburg. It seems likely that a ground search will be necessary. The CIA has provided you with a light aircraft. Underfly Janus radar and parachute in to the drop, proceed through the jungle eliminating any opposition and attempt to locate the Janus base.

M

Miss Simonova will be with you on this mission. If Janus is operating a Goldeneye satellite, her computing skills will be essential when you try to disable their systems. Jack Wade has given her some basic firearms training and he thinks she'll be able to carry her weight in a combat situation. Be careful though, 007, remember she isn't an experienced operative like yourself.

Q

You'll know you're on the right trail if you encounter Janus troops or automated defenses. Be especially wary of their robotic drone guns. I'm giving you some triggered mines for their ammo dump, try not to waste them on silly things. Oh, and look out for that Xenia woman, 007, I think she's after you. You lucky devil.

Moneypenny

A hot date in the rainforest? With two women! Shame on you James.

Description

Jungle is the fifteenth level of GoldenEye 007 and the first level of the seventh mission. It takes place in the Cuban jungle near Janus' secret base hidden beneath an artificial lake. Bond and Natalya search for the Janus Base by air when a Janus missile shoots down their plane. The pair survive the crash, but find themselves outnumbered and outgunned by hostile Janus forces stationed in the jungle.

Jungle is the only level where Bond has an armed ally throughout the mission. Natalya carries a Cougar Magnum and is actually pretty good with it. She is still vulnerable to explosions and guards will occasionally target her if they don't see Bond, so care must be taken to protect her.

The level is about two thirds open area with tropical trees, ferns, and other plants, and one third cave. The open area is pretty linear with some clearings for Drone Guns. There is also a rope bridge towards the end of that section and one unmanned guard tower. The cave section twists upon itself and makes for close quarters intense combat.
