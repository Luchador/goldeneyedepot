Prerelease Bunker 1

Prerelease Media

Prerelease Screenshots

These first three shots are stills from a video shows at Shoshinkai (Nintendo Space World) 1996. You can see the video here. The relevant section is at 8:00.

Early media for Bunker 1 shows the level guarded by Jungle Commandos rather than Russian Soldiers like in final. I'm not sure why since the Russian Soldier existed in the game by this point.


Bond originally started with an unsilenced PP7. He also wore his navy suit which was the default before his other outfits were made or scripted to be used.


Interestingly we see the aimer on screen here, yet the KF7 does not appear to be zoomed in. It seems there was a period when the KF7 had no zoom.


This shot shows Bond's navy suit cuff rather than the tactical outfit.


This screenshot shows a couple crates were added later including a metal one right about where the player is standing. All the screens use the Bond silhouette as a placeholder.


This shot is partially obscured by others, but it shows that Bunker's reflective eye was probably working by early 1997.

This screenshot from MundoRare doesn't have any differences from the final game.
