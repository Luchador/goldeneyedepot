Prerelease Screenshots

A Bunker multiplayer match showing Sean Connery's Bond still in the game.

This and the following screenshots are from the defunct MundoRare website. This particular screenshot doesn't show any differences from the final game.

This screenshot looks like it was taken right after the previous and also shows no differences.

Another screenshot that appears to be taken a frame or two after the last.

Man down! But again no differences from the retail release.

Our prerelease player moves into the red hall. But wait, where is the drone gun!? They already existed in the game by the time this screenshot was taken, so this shows a devious developer decided to add one here later in development.

We can't quite tell if the drone gun in the nook existed. It's a bit too far to the right to see if it's there.

This image answers the question. No, the drone gun in the nook did not exist! Also notice how the magazine ammo count disappears entirely when the magazine is empty instead of reading 0.

Here we see proof that drone guns did in fact exist at this point in development. This feels like a natural spot for a drone gun with the high ceiling and support beam. The ones added later always felt a bit odd because of how low they are.

When I grabbed this screenshot in the final game the drone gun never seemed to rotate as far to the left as it does in the prerelease screenshot.

This is an interesting view from the jail. It seems the cells did not have doors yet. Natalya isn't here and I have a feeling she wasn't in the level at all yet. You can also see hanging green lamps in the cells. It's also kind of strange that there are three Arctic Commandos in here. You can certainly lure them here in the final game it seems more likely they existed in the cell area in prerelease.

Another jail screenshot showing there was no key holder.

This room had fewer crates in prerelease.

This is a nice view of the old KF7 model.
