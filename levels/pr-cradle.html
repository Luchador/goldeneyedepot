Prerelease Cradle

Overview

Developer Adrian Smith was the first to work on Cradle. Some years ago Dr. Doak related to me that Smith's version of Cradle was too much for the Nintendo 64 to handle and sadly had to be scrapped. We really do not know anything more about it than that. Karl Hilton stepped in and remade the level from scratch, probably fairly late in development.
