Prerelease Surface 2

Overview

Surface 1 received most of the prerelease attention. The lack of prerelease media for Surface 2 makes me suspect it was not created until fairly late in development. It probably would have been made more quickly than most levels since it reuses the Surface 1 backgroud.

Prerelease Media

Prerelease Screenshots

We have almost no prerelease media of Surface 2. This screenshot is from the July 1997 issue of Nintendo Power so although technically prerelease it looks very similar to final. The sky does look black in the prelrease screenshot but that could just be the poor image quality rather than a real difference.
