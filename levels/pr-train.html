Prerelease Train

Prerelease Media

Removed Window

Nintendo Power Issue #98 from July 1997 has one image of Train and it's a very interesting image. It shows that Trevelyan's room once had a pane of glass with a white crosshair marking on it. It is not known why the window was removed or why it was not replaced with standard breakable glass. This must have been a very late change since the game released the next month.
