Runway

Mission 1: Arkangelsk

Part iii: Runway

Objectives

  1. Find plane ignition key (A / SA / 00A)
  2. Destroy heavy gun emplacements (00A)
  3. Destroy missile battery (SA / 00A)
  4. Escape in plane (A / SA / 00A)

Background

The service runway is the obvious escape route from the Arkangelsk chemical weapons facility.

M

006 knew this was going to be a risky mission. His sacrifice should give you enough time to find an aircraft. Keep a cool head and you'll get out alive. Just be certain to knock out the heavy gun emplacements and missile battery or they'll swat you out of the air like a silly fly.

Q

No time for gadgets here, 007. Just improvise with what you can find close to hand.

Moneypenny

Grace under pressure, as always, James.

Description

Runway is the shortest level in the game for casual play. It is also one of the fastest levels for speedrunning with the current completion times for Agent, Secret Agent, and 00 Agent standing at 0:21, 0:22, and 0:27, respectively.

The main goal is to escape from the gas plant via a light aircraft. While there aren't that many stationary guards, there are Heavy Gun Emplacements and infinitely respawning guards who can pose a serious threat.

Thankfully the Soviets were kind enough to leave an unguarded Tank near the facility for Bond to commandeer.
