Training/Armory

Overview

It sounds like it would have been a really cool looking level, and this adds another confirmation that the idea was carried over to Perfect Dark. But there's also a couple of odd statements here. Mirroring the level's geometry to create a reflection shouldn't have been too much of an issue for the N64. They did it for Egyptian's reflecting pool after all. And if cartridge space was an issue why not remove Citadel to make room for the shooting range?

Assets

It can't be said for certain, but I think this texture was likely meant to be the shooting range target.

We do not have the level's main geometry, but data miners did find an unused collision tile file in the XBLA leak. The file goes by the name "sho", perhaps short for shooting range. It consists of a hexagonal center with three branches. We believe this is likely the collision for the cut level. See below for an interactive view. The three branches match well with Smith's description of the three ranges.
