Magazines

Goldeneye 007 had some coverage in gaming magazines prior to its release, although not as much as we would like. It was really after the game released that magazines really picked up on it. This page covers GoldenEye articles from the first articles in March 1995 up through the end of 1997 and a couple beyond. The list is organized in chronological order.

Total! - Issue 39 - March 1995

This is either the first or second mention of a GoldenEye video game in a magazine. Disappointingly it really gives no detail other than to optimistically predict GoldenEye would be released later the same year.

Total! Issue 39 on the Internet Archive

Super Gamer - Issue 12 - March 1995

This blurb on the bottom left gives a little more detail than the last. The author correctly claims that GoldenEye would use Pierce Brosnan's likeness, although they were under the impression it would be a 2D Super Nintendo game.

Super Gamer Issue 12 on the Internet Archive

Nintendo Power - Volume 71 - April 1995

This is the first in-depth magazine article on GoldenEye the video game. The article describes GoldenEye as a Super Nintendo game, although we know that project lead Martin Hollis with pitching the game for the "Ultra 64" around this time. "Ultra 64" was the early name of the Nintendo 64. The article writer was under the impression that GoldenEye would make use of prerendered sprites in a similar fashion to Donkey Kong Country. I'm sure this was not intentional misinformation, but just the result of Nintendo Power being unaware that GoldenEye would be a Nintendo 64 game.

Nintendo Power Volume 71 on the Internet Archive

Nintendo Magazine System (AU) - Issue #25 - April 1995

This issue of Nintendo Magazine System, the official Nintendo magazine for Australia, also came out in April 1995. Like Nintendo Power, they believed GoldenEye would be a SNES game. I'm not sure why the article claims Pierce Brosnan is an American actor. He is actually from Ireland.

Nintendo Magazine System Issue #25 on the Internet Archive

Hyper - Issue #19 - June 1995

There is a small amount of information on GoldenEye in the upper right column. Like the magazines before, Hyper was under the impression GoldenEye would be a 2D platformer.

Hyper Issue #19 on the Internet Archive

Maximum - Issue #3 - January 1996

One of the first articles to reveal that GoldenEye would be a fully 3D game for the Ultra 64. It features two screenshots from the Shoshinkai 1995 trade show which was held from November 24 to November 26 in Tokyo, the first public demonstration of GoldenEye.
