GoldenEye Sites and Accounts

GoldenEye Setup Editor

This is the ultimate tool for modding Goldeneye 007 and digging into its files. This website would have been 10x more difficult to make without it and some things would have been impossible.

Get the Setup Editor here!

The-Elite.net

This is the long established home of Goldeneye 007 speedrunning, i.e. trying to beat levels as fast as possible. If you're even thinking about speedrunning GoldenEye, look here.

Visit their website.

GoldenEye: Decoded

Spectacular blog for a look into the game's development and legacy. Unfortunately it is no longer updated but it still has a lot of great info.

Visit GoldenEye: Decoded.

Graslu00 on YouTube

High resolution and informative videos of walkthroughs, challenges, mods, custom levels and more! All-around great guy, too, who has done a lot to keep the Goldeneye 007 community thriving.

Check out his channel here.

Shooters Forever Forums

Forum for Goldeneye 007 and other retro shooter games. Not that active these days but contains a treasure trove of GoldenEye information.

Visit the forums here.

Dr. Doak on X

Goldeneye 007 developer Dr. David Doak is active on X! He's a truly amazing person and I highly recommend following him.

Dr. Doak's X profile.

AdzyIn3D on YouTube

Relatively new and awesome Goldeneye 007 YouTube channel with videos of custom levels, mods, and tier lists.

Visit AdzyIn3D's channel.

GoldenEye: Source

Total conversion mod for Half-Life 2 that remakes Goldeneye 007's multiplayer. I worked on it for many years so I must admit I have some bias in favor of it. It's not too populated these days but there's still a group who plays it every Saturday night/Sunday morning (US time zones) and you're welcome to join in.

Get the mod here.

Goose's Gamer Folklore

Impressive YouTube channel from Goldeneye 007 speedrunner and speedrun historian Ryan White covers breaking developments in the world of GoldenEye speedrunning, among many other interesting topics.

Visit his channel here. Also check out his other channel, Speedlore, where Ryan goes into great detail about the history of GoldenEye speedrunning.

The GoldenEye Dossier

This is site is mostly about GoldenEye the movie (which is interesting in and of itself), but also has a great section for the game. There's also a fantastic account on X with lots of movie trivia.

The GoldenEye Depot is an unofficial fan website administered by Ben Colclough. It is not affiliated with Nintendo, Rare, MGM, or EON.
